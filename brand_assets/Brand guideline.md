 Nrhoot Brand Guidelines                                                                                                                   
  Brand Identity

  App Name: Nrhoot — a produce/community marketplace mobile app (React
   Native / Expo).
  Mascot: Lemon welcome mascot (friendly, approachable tone).
  Design Language: Follows iOS Human Interface Guidelines with a      
  fresh, nature-inspired feel.

  ---
  Color Palette

  Primary Brand Colors
  - Primary Green: #20C897
  - Secondary Green: #249689
  - Mint Accent: #A8E6CF
  - Splash / Highlight Mint: #a8e6cf
  - Positive/Success Green: #58BD52
  - Secondary Accent: #19DB8A

  Backgrounds
  - Main Background: #E7F7F4
  - Surface / Card Background: #BBEFE1
  - Secondary Background: #F2F2F7
  - White Card: #FFFFFF

  Text
  - Primary Text: #000000
  - Secondary Text: #3C3C43
  - Tertiary Text / Placeholder: #8E8E93
  - Dark Element: #14181B

  System / Utility Colors
  - Border: #E5E5EA
  - Error / Destructive: #FF3B30
  - Success: #34C759
  - Warning: #FF9500
  - Info: #5AC8FA
  - Star Rating Gold: #FFD700

  Gray Scale
  #8E8E93 → #AEAEB2 → #C7C7CC → #D1D1D6 → #E5E5EA → #F2F2F7

  ---
  Logo

  - Primary logo: nrhoot-logo.png (full color)
  - On dark/colored backgrounds: nrhoot-logo-white.png
  - Transparent background variant: nrhoot-logo-nobg.png
  - App icon: icon.png
  - The logo is used in headers, splash screens, and onboarding. Never
   distort or recolor the logo outside the approved variants.

  ---
  Icons

  - Library: @expo/vector-icons v15
  - Primary set: Ionicons (used throughout the app)
  - Secondary set: MaterialIcons (supplementary)
  - Tab bar: Emoji icons — 🏠 Home, 🔍 Find, ➕ Post, 💬 Messages, 👥 
  Community, 👤 Profile

  Common icon usage:
  - Navigation: arrow-back, settings, close-circle
  - Actions: add, trash, pencil, share-outline
  - Social: heart, heart-outline, bookmark, chat-bubble-outline       
  - Location: location, near-me, place
  - Status: check-circle, star, rate-review
